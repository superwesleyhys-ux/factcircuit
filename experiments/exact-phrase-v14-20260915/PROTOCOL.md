# Literal source tracing: fixed comparison conditions

This diagnostic tests a new no-summary item path, not whether a harness outperforms a raw model in general. The baseline and candidate share the literal extraction, request runner, citation validator and result schema. The only arm difference is additional tracing guidance. Thus this isolates that guidance within the shared runner; it does not compare every feature of FactCircuit against a bare chat UI.

The real event is the October 2022 DART orbital-change announcement. The input is the complete text-node extraction of the original Axios article from a verified 2023 archive capture. The source pool contains that raw capture and its actually linked NASA announcement, reached through verified historical redirects to a December 2024 capture. Original HTML bytes, metadata and extraction hashes are retained locally. No hand-written story summaries or claim reformulations enter model input.

Before inference, freeze the code, two raw captures and metadata, policies, three item locations, model and budgets. Items are `32 minutes`, `73 seconds`, and the literal `if` in the success-threshold sentence. Exact phrases select every occurrence; the single word is chosen by its exact occurrence offset. These three items belong to ONE event, not three independent test events. This is a development diagnostic; the developer has read the documents. It is not an unseen holdout or a 2025–2026 fabrication prediction test.

Both arms use real `gpt-6-astra`, low reasoning, through isolated local Codex login (remote inference). Both have three calls per item, four document attempts including the seed, the same complete document capacity, the same schema, and no retries. The archived two-document pool is identical and frozen for both. Each item/arm initially receives only the news page; opening a source follows an actual hyperlink. Search is disabled in this controlled replay, clearly visible in limits. This does not measure unrestricted search or a paywall bypass. The production live collector separately supports bounded public search. Actual calls and token totals may differ and will be reported, not equalized after the fact. Arm order is fixed direct then harness, not randomized.

Each item is isolated from all previous item judgments. Every call receives the full unchanged available document texts plus exact item offsets, hashes, context and exact-match navigation. Retrieval history contains actions and receipts only, not summaries or model reasons. A missing phrase in an origin is not proof of contradiction; semantic equivalence must be assessed against its actual surrounding sentence. The model may quote only exact occurrences; the program computes citation offsets. Invalid outputs and blocked fetches remain explicit failures or evidence gaps. No repair or policy change after seeing results.

Report (1) exact input/citation integrity, (2) actual upstream retrieval and observed chain, (3) whether observation, success threshold and conditional wording are distinguished, (4) unresolved/invalid judgments and missing evidence, and (5) measured usage. Do not call phrase match rates factual accuracy. Do not call a NASA announcement independent authentication of underlying telescope records. `if` is absent in NASA's threshold sentence; an unrelated `if` elsewhere must not be laundered into equivalent wording. These are predeclared review questions; no reference labels enter the runner.

Preserve all results regardless of outcome. A consistency review may identify unsupported model interpretations but must not edit the raw predictions. Neither a successful citation check nor finding the original speaker guarantees world truth. HTTP/public text availability does not certify all visual content. Access-restricted pages are excluded; membership is not bypassed.
